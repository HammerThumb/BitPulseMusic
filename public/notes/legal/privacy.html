Privacy Policy

Sticky Planner — a product of HammerThumb Studios LLC

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

The short version: Sticky Planner runs entirely in your browser. Your notes, boards, images, and settings are stored only on your device (in the browser's IndexedDB and localStorage). We do not operate a server for your content, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your notes. We can't see them.

1. Who we are

Sticky Planner ("the App") is provided by HammerThumb Studios LLC ("we", "us", "our"). For any privacy question or request, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), HammerThumb Studios LLC is the data controller for the limited processing described below.

2. What data the App handles, and where it lives

Your content stays on your device. Everything you create in the App — notes, boards/tabs, embedded images, themes, and preferences — is written to your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on the machine and browser profile where you use the App. This content:

  • is not sent to us or to any third party;
  • is not accessible to us — we have no server that receives or stores it;
  • is removed if you clear your browser's site data, or by using the App's delete controls; and
  • can be moved by you at any time using the App's Backup / Restore (export and import) feature, which produces a file you control.

Because content is stored per browser, opening the App in a different browser or profile shows a separate board. We never receive a copy in the process.

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics or telemetry SDKs, fingerprinting, or third-party trackers. It does not create user accounts and does not require you to provide a name, email address, or any personal information to use it.

We do not retain your content because we never receive it; you control its lifetime on your device. Any Premium license/order records are retained by the Storefront and by us only as needed to provide Premium, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.
